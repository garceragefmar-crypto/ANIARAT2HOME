About Us

AniArat2Home is an online platform created to connect farmers and consumers through a simple and accessible marketplace.

Our goal is to provide farmers with an opportunity to sell their agricultural products directly to consumers while making fresh farm products easier to access. By reducing dependence on intermediaries, AniArat2Home aims to create a more direct connection between the people who grow our food and the people who buy it.

Mission

Our mission is to connect local farmers directly with consumers through a convenient and reliable platform for selling and purchasing fresh agricultural products, while creating better opportunities for farmers to reach customers and increase their income.

Vision

To become a trusted platform that connects local farmers and consumers, promotes local agriculture, and brings fresh farm products closer to every home.
